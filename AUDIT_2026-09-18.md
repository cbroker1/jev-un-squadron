4. Next observed state: frame 20257 reports `applied_action=up`, Y `77`, and Y button active. The next Jev request again chooses `up`.

The controller therefore did receive and apply a real Jev choice. The Y fire pulse and the five-call limit are deterministic bridge behavior, not Jev decisions. The prompt’s target-70 policy is still calibration, and its input field is not the verified player Y.

## Direction check

- The plumbing can genuinely control the aircraft and fire it, both deterministically and through a real Jev Choice response.
- Today’s useful conclusions are the verified player coordinates/directions, visible Y firing, emulated-frame timing, reproducible danger/death observation, and a working bounded live smoke path.
- The biggest blocker is the absence of a validated enemy/bullet state and health/damage observation. Without that, Jev cannot receive compact evidence about imminent threats; raw statistical RAM candidates are not enough.
- The experiments are beginning to answer specific questions, but the hazard phase has produced many one-off scripts, large full-WRAM files, and repeated candidate scans without a promoted object mapping. The next experiment must have a single identity test and a pass/fail rule.
- Unattended launch is repeatable for bounded runs, but forceful process cleanup and the incomplete mid-run reload test should be treated as infrastructure caveats.
- Screenshots are useful for the DANGER/death boundary, gunfire, and offline coordinate annotations. BizHawk’s saved screenshots omit Lua GUI drawings, so they cannot validate the live marker/overlay. The annotated images are offline evidence, not proof that the Lua overlay was visible in the emulator.
- Jev cannot yet observe threats well enough for useful avoidance. The deterministic fixed-target and dry-run policies must not be reported as Jev gameplay.
- Scope has expanded into many exploratory launchers and full-WRAM captures. Consolidation should wait until one hazard identity is validated; power-ups, shops, special weapons, and collision geometry remain correctly deferred.
