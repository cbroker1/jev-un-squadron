# Audit - 2026-09-20, terrain extraction and the decision pipeline

Scope: verify the previous session's claims against artifacts. Three probes used:
a rendered screenshot with tile grids (`tile_grid.json`, `tile_grid_frame.png`), a full
64 KB VRAM dump with its screenshot (`vram_full.txt`, `vram_full_frame.png`), and the
scrolling tilemap capture already on disk (`tilemap_samples.jsonl`).

## 1. The stage layer was misidentified; the evidence points to 0x9800, not 0xA000

**Claim under test:** "VRAM 0xA000 is the stage layer - that's the layer your idea needs."

**Verdict: unsupported.** At frame 22212, scroll 1054, with ground and structures plainly
on screen (`tile_grid_frame.png`), the 0xA000 grid is **empty for every row below y160**,
which is exactly the band where all recorded terrain collisions happen (Y 160-191). Its
two visible features also cannot share one vertical offset: the ridge implies about +61 px
and the tower pattern about +3 px, so the "constant offset" idea fails on its own data.

**What the evidence does support:** fitting occupancy against 68 labelled cells (shot-stop
= solid, shot-pass = open) across 130 scroll positions:

| layer | vertical offset | horizontal offset | solid cells occupied | open cells occupied |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **0x9800** | **32** | **-8** | **65%** | **7%** |
| 0x9800 | 32 | +32 | 57% | 7% |
| 0x A000 | 232 | 0 | 67% | 34% |

0x9800 at (32, -8) separates solid from open by 57 points, about 9:1. 0xA000's best fit
looks similar on recall but marks a third of open cells as solid, which is the failure
mode that collapsed R33 to 9 kills.

**Uncertainty:** 65% recall, not 100%. The 398 solid observations come from only ~34
independent cells. This tests *occupancy* (tile index non-zero), not which tiles collide.
One level only.

**Smallest fix:** a reader that returns occupancy from 0x9800 with those constants, used
only to *add* structure knowledge alongside the existing shot-stop map, never to veto a
firing line until the acceptance check passes.

**Acceptance check:** on a run not used for fitting, >=85% of recorded collision cells and
<=10% of shot-pass cells are occupied. Below that, the mapping is still wrong.

## 2. Tile indices are level-specific; "solid in any level" is wrong

**Claim under test:** "once a tile index is known solid, it's solid everywhere it appears,
in every level."

**Verdict: wrong, and it matters for the labyrinth levels.** Tile indices address a tileset
that is streamed into VRAM per level: 0xD800 changes as the stage scrolls (64 bytes between
dumps 60 px apart) and its contents are dense pseudo-random bytes, which is tile pixel data
being paged in, not a map. The same index in another level indexes different art.

**Smallest fix:** key any learned solidity by (level, tile index), or rely on occupancy
only, which is a property of the map rather than of the tileset.

**Acceptance check:** dump CHR for two levels and confirm the same index renders different
graphics before any cross-level claim is made.

## 3. Structure coverage was reported 3.7x too high

`terrain_map.json` today holds **34 cells across 17 columns**, not the 63 columns quoted to
Carl. The 63 predates the pass-clearance fix, which correctly deleted cells contradicted by
shots flying through them. The number in the handoff and in chat is stale.

**Fix:** correct the docs; regenerate the figure from the artifact rather than from memory.
**Acceptance:** the documented count equals `len(columns with shots_stopped_at)`.

## 4. Nothing carries between decisions

The request contains no record of the previous choice, the current objective, or any
commitment: searching `data/samples/jev_request_example.json` for "previous", "last choice",
"committed" and "plan" returns nothing. Every decision is judged fresh.

Measured consequence: 16-22 immediate direction reversals per run while something sits
behind the aircraft, and a 40-decision stutter in run 73 that survived several rounds of
added facts. Multi-move commitments (transit to a target behind, an approach to a power-up)
are computed per option but cannot be sustained, because the next request has no memory of
having started one.

**Smallest fix:** add the last three choices and the objective being pursued to `state`.
**Acceptance:** reversals while a target is behind fall by >=30% over three runs with
kills-per-unit-met no worse.

## 5. Instruction mass, and four competing priority claims

Each call sends ~22 KB: 6,489 characters of instructions plus five option descriptions of
~1,600 each. Inside the instructions at least four priorities compete without an ordering:
turrets are "the highest-value target", a power-up "is worth more than an ordinary kill",
a screen-clearing power-up "reshapes the rest", and the squeeze guidance. Separately, four
instructions written by the previous agent each lost kills when added (36, 41, 37 and 13
kills) and were reverted, while every measured fact helped.

**Smallest fix:** A/B a trimmed instruction block that keeps measured facts and Carl's own
guidance and drops the rest.
**Acceptance:** kills-per-unit-met not worse across three runs with request size down >=30%.

## Follow-up: the VRAM terrain thread is closed, negative

Pursued to conclusion after the audit, because Carl reported a third failure of the same
kind (run 79, d470: firing into a land feature). At that decision the aircraft was at
level x 1717 and the structure map held nothing there, so the shot geometry saw a clear
line. That is a coverage gap, not a reasoning error.

Attempts, each validated rather than assumed:

| approach | result |
|---|---|
| 0xA000 occupancy, offsets fitted on shot stops | fails held-out collisions at 10% |
| 0xA000 refitted on collisions (320 combinations, 26 cells) | 81% / 16%, fails the bar and looks like overfitting |
| deep replay capture, full level, 0x9800 occupancy | 47% of stops, 7% of passes; 31% false refusals when used for firing |
| learning which tile indices are solid | 47 of 89 stop observations land on tile 0x0000, empty |
| solving alignment against a rendered screenshot, ~900 configurations | best 92%, beaten by a null model using the row alone at 93% |

The last line is the decisive one: the tilemap adds nothing over "sky above, content below"
at any alignment reachable this way. Resolving it needs the PPU's own BG scroll and base
registers rather than inferred offsets, which the Snes9x core here does not expose; a core
that does (bsnes, Mesen) would settle it in one capture.

Probe scripts are kept (`lua/probe_tilemap.lua` now supports replaying a run's inputs, which
is what finally gave whole-level coverage). The large capture files are deleted; they are
reproducible from the scripts.

## Follow-up: the structure map was retuned on measured trade-offs

The firing rule's evidence was cleared over 4 px, leaving 19 columns and catching 15.7% of
shots that hit terrain for 2.0% false refusals. Measured over 203 shots that destroyed
something and 172 that hit terrain, clearing over 2 px instead leaves 24 columns and
catches 22.1% for 6.4%. That is the better operating point while Carl is watching shots go
into structures, and it is far from the 30% false-refusal rate that made the aircraft stop
attacking in R33.
